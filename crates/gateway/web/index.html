Live demo · paper money

A real matching engine. Pretend money.

Every order you place here goes through a working exchange: it is numbered, written to a journal, matched against the bots' orders, and settled in your paper account. Nothing on this page involves real funds.

  • Price-time priorityBest price first, then first come, first served. Limit, immediate-or-cancel and post-only orders here; the engine also does icebergs, stops and auctions.
  • Nothing confirmed that could be lostCommands are journaled and synced to disk before anyone hears about them.
  • Crash recoveryAfter a restart, checkpoints and the journal bring back every balance and every open order.
  • Built for low latencyOne deterministic matching thread that allocates nothing while it trades, and an optional pipeline of threads joined by lock-free rings.
  • Checked, not just testedProperty tests against a reference model, fuzzing, Miri, loom, Kani proofs and mutation testing in CI.
  • Nothing hiddenSwitch the book to Orders to see every order in its queue, yours among them. Scroll down to the engine room for every command as it is sequenced, and the server's latency second by second.
