The native benchmark's baseline flow from 64 participants: limit orders near the touch, aggressive limits, market orders, cancels and modifies. 200,000 commands warm the book up to thousands of resting orders, then 500,000 are timed, five rounds.

There is more: a whole exchange around it

The order book is one crate of an exchange: a write-ahead journal synced before anything is acknowledged, crash recovery that rebuilds the exact state, a pipeline of pinned threads joined by lock-free rings, a gateway for programs and browsers, and a demo that trades paper money against a live mirror of Bitstamp's ETH/USD order book.
